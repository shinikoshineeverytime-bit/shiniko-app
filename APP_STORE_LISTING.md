## Full Description

### App Store / Play Store Description

**Get your car washed anywhere, anytime.**

Shiniko connects you with professional car washers who come to your location. No more waiting at car washes – just book, and a washer comes to you.

**🚗 FOR CAR OWNERS**
• Book an exterior car wash in seconds
• £25 flat rate – no hidden fees
• Washer comes to your location
• Track your booking in real-time
• Message your washer directly
• Secure payment via card

**💧 FOR CAR WASHERS**
• Earn money on your own schedule
• Accept jobs near you
• Get paid after each wash
• Easy-to-use washer dashboard
• Direct messaging with customers

**HOW IT WORKS**
1. Open the app and tap "Get a wash"
2. Enter your car registration and colour
3. Confirm booking and pay £25
4. A nearby washer accepts your job
5. Your car gets washed – done!

**PRICING**
• Exterior Car Wash: £25
• No subscription required
• Pay per wash

**SAFE & SECURE**
• Verified washers
• Secure Stripe payments
• In-app messaging
• Real-time tracking

Download Shiniko today and never wait at a car wash again!
