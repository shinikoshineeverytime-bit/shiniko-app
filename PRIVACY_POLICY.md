# Privacy Policy for Shiniko

**Last Updated: March 2025**

## Introduction

Shiniko ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Shiniko mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our car wash booking service.

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide
- **Name**: To identify you to washers/customers
- **Location**: To show your position for car wash services
- **Vehicle Information**: Registration number and colour to help washers identify your car
- **Payment Information**: Processed securely through Stripe (we don't store card details)

### Information Collected Automatically
- **Device Information**: Device type and operating system
- **Usage Data**: App interactions and features used

## How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:
- Connect customers with car washers
- Process payments securely
- Enable in-app messaging between customers and washers
- Improve our services

## Payment Processing

All payments are processed securely through Stripe. We never store your full card details. Stripe's privacy policy applies to payment processing: https://stripe.com/privacy

## Data Sharing

We share your information only with:
- **Washers/Customers**: Name and location (for service delivery)
- **Stripe**: For payment processing
- **Law Enforcement**: If legally required

We do **not** sell your personal information.

## Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your information, including encryption and secure data storage.

## Your Rights

You can:
- Request access to your data
- Request deletion of your account
- Opt out of marketing communications

## Children's Privacy

Shiniko is not intended for users under 18 years old.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. We will notify you of significant changes through the app.

For questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at:
